FW Workspace – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

FW Workspace is a private team workspace used by FW Studio to manage video editing tasks, briefs and file deliveries. This page explains what data the app uses.

What we access

  • Your Google account email, only to know which account the workspace is connected to.
  • Google Drive files created by this app. The app uses the drive.file permission, which only allows it to create and manage files it uploads itself. It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.

How we use it

Files uploaded by team members through the workspace (videos, project files, exports) are stored in a single Google Drive folder owned by the workspace administrator. They are used only to deliver and review work inside the workspace.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not read any Drive files that the app did not create.

Storage and security

The access permission is stored securely on a private server and is never shown to other users. Uploaded files remain in the administrator's Google Drive and can be deleted by the administrator at any time.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
